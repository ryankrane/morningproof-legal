Frequently Asked Questions

How does AI photo verification work?

Simply take a photo of your completed habit and our AI will instantly verify it. The AI analyzes the image to confirm your habit is actually done, so you can't cheat your way to a streak.

How do I set up app blocking?

Go to Settings and tap on App Blocking. You'll be prompted to grant Screen Time permissions, then you can select which apps to block until your morning habits are complete.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases". Your subscription is linked to your Apple ID and will be restored automatically.

Can I change my morning cutoff time?

Yes! Go to Settings and adjust your cutoff time. This is the deadline by which you need to complete your morning habits each day.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Morning Proof to cancel or modify your subscription.

My apps aren't unlocking after I complete my habits

Make sure your habit was verified by AI (you should see a checkmark). If issues persist, try restarting the app or contact us at the email above.
